04

Checks performed

  • Sorts holes by center position and detects duplicate centers.
  • Computes center-to-center spacing and edge-to-edge gap for adjacent holes.
  • Flags edge gaps below the user-defined fabrication threshold.
  • Flags abrupt adjacent diameter changes above the user-defined taper threshold.

Engineering note: this is a geometry pre-check, not a substitute for CAD/EBL tool validation, proximity-effect correction, process-bias calibration, or microscope/metrology review.
